**Status:** "Confirmed" means stated in the official Eurostat notes. "Reported" means from a third-party source. "Unverified" means no source yet.

**How to verify:** use UN Comtrade with reporter = the country and partner = the destination, for the same year and product codes. Record the year and the codes used.
